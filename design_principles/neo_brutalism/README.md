## The 5 Non-Negotiable Laws of Neo-Brutalism

1. **Exposed Ink Borders (`2px`–`4px solid #0C0C0E`):** Every container, button, input, and badge is explicitly framed by an unbroken high-contrast ink stroke.
2. **Zero-Blur Hard Offset Shadows (`0px` blur, `0px` spread, `100%` opacity):** Blurred drop shadows (`0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`) are strictly prohibited. All shadows are sharp geometric cutouts (`3px 3px 0`, `5px 5px 0`, `8px 8px 0`).
3. **Invariant Translation + Shadow Coupling ($\Delta\text{translate} + \Delta\text{shadow} = \text{constant}$):** Interactive controls hover by lifting up-left (`translate(-3px, -3px)` with `8px 8px 0` shadow) and press by slamming into their shadow footprint (`translate(5px, 5px)` with `0px 0px 0` shadow).
4. **Warm Paper Canvas + High-Chroma Flat Fills:** Anchor `60%–70%` of the viewport in Warm Cream (`#FAF6EE`) or Putty (`#EFECE6`) so crisp `#FFFFFF` cards and high-chroma accents (`#FFE600`, `#FF52A2`, `#23F0C7`, `#A8FF35`) pop without eye strain.
5. **Strict Black Ink on Bright Pop Accents (`> 10:1` AAA Contrast):** Always use `#0C0C0E` ink on Yellow, Cyan, Lime, Mint, and Pastel surfaces—never white text.
